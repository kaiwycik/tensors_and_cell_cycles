- Created `playground_3.ipynb`: a single-cell-line scITD replica on T47D, using a treatment x cell-cycle phase x protein tensor (8 x 3 x 22 after preprocessing).
	- Imported T47D data, filtered out pRB/cyto features, excluded G0 and M phases, and subset to 22 cell-cycle-related proteins.
	- Pseudobulked by (treatment, phase), z-scored, and reshaped into the 3-mode tensor.
	- Implemented HOSVD with truncated ranks; selected protein rank via the maximum-gap-from-interpolation-line heuristic (landed on 8 factors).
- Built out Section 3 (visualizations):
	- Protein loadings heatmap and per-factor lollipop plots showing signed weights.
	- Constructed the effect tensor $\mathcal{F} = \mathcal{G} \times_1 T \times_2 C$ and produced treatment-averaged and phase-averaged heatmaps of the latent protein-factors, plus a three-panel phase-slice montage of $\mathcal{F}$.
- Built out Section 4 (understanding latent protein-factors):
	- Computed a PHATE embedding of 10k subsampled cells and colored it by treatment, cell-cycle phase, and dominant protein.
	- Projected single-cell data onto the protein-factor space ($\mathbf{X} \cdot \mathbf{P}$) and colored PHATE by each of the 8 factor scores.
	- Added "archetypal cells" analysis: per-treatment PHATE embeddings with cosine-distance-to-factor violin plots, split by phase.
	- Visualized dominant latent protein-factor per treatment.
