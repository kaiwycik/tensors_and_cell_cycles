- We are then interested in"\:
	- What are the cell cycles that remain unchanged under treatment?
	- What is the distribution of the cell cycles across the treatments?
	- How do the treatments affect the cell cycles?

	- What mode of variation do the components of the Treatment loadings capture, when we consider the other "dimensions"?

$$
